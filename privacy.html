Pawma: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Pawma ("we", "us") is an owner-held pet health record. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Questions: swagwithus@thewaglabco.com.

Who we are

Pawma is operated by The Wag Lab Co., Thailand. The data controller is The Wag Lab Co.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and authentication credentials.
  • Pet health records you create: pets' names and details, vet visits, vaccinations, lab results, care recommendations, and daily journal entries (meals, behavior, symptoms). This can include animal health information.
  • Media you capture: photos of lab reports and documents, pet photos, and audio recordings of vet visits, where you choose to add them.
  • Basic technical data: device and app version needed to run the service.
  • Service activity: limited events such as record creation, document extraction, voice structuring, and Care sharing activity. Product analytics use aggregate counts and do not include names, emails, notes, transcripts, lab values, document paths, pictures, or audio.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How we use it

  • To store and display your pet's health record for you.
  • To transcribe recordings on your iPhone and structure captured information into your record.
  • To send optional local reminders before a vaccine or recheck is due (on-device).
  • To operate, secure, and support the app.
  • To understand aggregate product adoption and feature use so we can improve Pawma.

We do not use the content of veterinary records, notes, transcripts, pictures, or audio for general product analytics.

Processing of your captures

Vet-visit recordings are transcribed on your iPhone. Pawma requires on-device recognition, which prevents the recording from being sent over the network for transcription. The original recording is uploaded only to Pawma's private Supabase storage when you choose to attach it to a visit. Short field dictation is held only in the app cache and deleted after the words are added to the field.

When you ask Pawma to organize captured information, the following content is sent to our processor solely to structure it into an editable record:

  • OpenAI: structures lab documents and the text transcript produced on your iPhone. Pawma does not send visit audio to OpenAI.

OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default. Standard API abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days unless a longer period is legally required. Only record what you are comfortable capturing, and obtain consent before recording other people (e.g. your veterinarian).

Where your data is stored

Records and files are stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase (Postgres database and file storage). Per-user access rules prevent one Pawma user from accessing another user's account data. A limited number of authorized Pawma administrators may access records only when necessary to operate, secure, support, recover, or comply with legal obligations for the service. Product analytics are provided to administrators only as aggregate counts. Pet photos are stored for display within the app.

Sharing

We share data only with the processors above (Supabase and OpenAI) to run the service, or where required by law. We never sell it.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your records, files, and account (see below).

Your choices & deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, in-app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your records, photos, audio, and account and cannot be undone. You can also email swagwithus@thewaglabco.com to request deletion or a copy of your data.

Children

Pawma is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this policy as the product evolves and note the date above.
