Common questions

  • Reset your password: tap Forgot password? on the email sign-in screen and request a new six-digit code.
  • A lab or visit was read incorrectly: review and correct structured information before saving. You can also edit saved information in the app.
  • Delete your account and data: open Settings, Account, Continue to delete account. This action cannot be undone. See the Privacy Policy for backup retention.
