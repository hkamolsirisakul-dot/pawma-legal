Common questions

  • Reset your password — on the sign-in screen, contact us and we'll send a reset link.
  • A lab or visit was read incorrectly — you can edit any record in-app; tap the record to correct it. Tell us too, so we can improve.
  • Delete your account and data — in the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your records, photos, audio, and account and cannot be undone.
